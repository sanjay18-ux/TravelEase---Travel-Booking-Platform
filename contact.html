Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding bookings, tickets, and refunds.

How do I receive my booking confirmation & e-tickets?

Once your reservation is confirmed, your e-tickets and confirmed PNR vouchers are instantly delivered to your email and accessible under "Manage Booking" in your TravelEase account.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Most hotels and select flights offer Free Cancellation up to 24-48 hours before check-in or departure. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days with zero TravelEase convenience fee deductions.

Can I customize a featured holiday package?

Yes! All packages can be tailored to add extra nights, upgrade to private pool villas, or incorporate specialized activities like scuba diving, hot air ballooning, or private sightseeing cabs.
